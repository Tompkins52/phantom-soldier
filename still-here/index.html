The story behind the song

This was the first one.

Every song on this site is sung in a voice that was built from this track. Before there was a project, there was one plain description of what a lot of veterans live with after they come home, and almost never say out loud.

The house is quiet, and the quiet is the problem. Ears that ring all night from years of noise. Three nights without sleep, and then the best sleep in years in the seat of a vehicle with sand blowing through it, because for a long time that's where sleeping made sense. The job that calls you a problem. People you love who can't understand why you can't just switch it off. And the part nobody can see, the one that locks the door from the inside. The longest tour is the one in your head.

The quiet in this houseis the loudest thing I keep.

from Still Here

It was never meant to be a sad song. It was meant to be the stance. Everything stacked against you, and you're still here. Not winning, not fixed. Still here, and never learned to quit. That sentence is the reason this site exists, and it's why we tell people to start with this track.

I'm still here.I never learned to quit.

from Still Here

One thing to listen for

There's a high ringing tone at the very start and again at the very end, after the last chord. That's the tinnitus. If you have it, you already knew.

More stories behind the songs

Every song on this site started as one true thing. No names go on the songs, ever. If you've got a story, it costs nothing, you tell it your way, and everything the music earns goes to veteran suicide prevention.
