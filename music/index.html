The Same Right Hand

Debut EP, July 2026. Five songs, 22 minutes.

We weren't going to ask anybody for their story until we'd gone first. The first five songs are built from our own experience and from what we've learned from other veterans, written so more people can see themselves in them. If you're deciding whether to tell yours, listen to these first. They're the honest answer to what we'll do with it. The stories behind Still Here and All the Wrong Reasons are up.

Open the EP in Spotify or YouTube Music. Also on Apple Music and Amazon Music. Share Still Here: phantomsoldier.org/still-here

Full disclosure, because you'll wonder when you hit play: these songs are made with AI. The lyrics are drafted with AI's help and the music is produced with Suno. The stories are real, and a real person works with you from start to finish.

Phantom Soldier

Veteran stories, told as songs. You tell the story you want told. If it becomes one, it's released under Phantom Soldier. No veteran's name is ever on it. No cost to the veteran, and everything the music earns is donated to veteran suicide prevention.
