It started as a drawing.

One page: a path going up, lines across it, and a band of X's at the bottom. Every line is a wall of glass, and the thicker the line, the harder it is to break. The X's are where you land when one doesn't give. It's a picture of depression, and you don't have to be a veteran to recognize it. The whole drawing, next to the lines it turned into, is on the song's page.
