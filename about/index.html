Who's behind this

One Army veteran, a day job, and no plan. Here's the honest version.

I started this because of my own stuff. Depression, suicidal thoughts, the manic stretches, all of it. Until about five years ago I didn't believe any of that was the real problem. It was.

The songs so far are built from my own experience and from what I've learned from other veterans, put into one song so more people can relate. You won't find whose story a song came from anywhere on this site. That's the rule for everybody.

Here's what I noticed when people heard them. A civilian listens and it's just another song. A veteran listens and you can see it land. That's the connection: we all know what we're not talking about. The VA has built a lot of real resources, and they matter. But sometimes it's veterans who need to help veterans.

I've sat in group therapy with other veterans and watched the frustration on their faces, because they're lost. There are veterans out there who have been through things I can't even imagine. The whole point of this site is to reach them and connect them.

I had no idea I could do this. I didn't go in with a plan. It might fail. It definitely won't make money, and I don't care about money.

I don't even play an instrument. But the music helps, and I don't know why. If it helps one person, it's worth doing.

I'm here for anybody who's willing to take the help. All I ask is that you share it. If it helped you, it might help somebody else.

Mark Tompkins
Army veteran. Founder, Phantom Soldier.

Veteran stories, told as songs. You tell the story you want told. If it becomes one, it's released under Phantom Soldier or kept private, just for you. No cost to the veteran, and everything the music earns is donated to veteran suicide prevention.
