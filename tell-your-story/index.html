What makes a story easy to write from

You don't have to write well. That part is on us. What helps is detail.

  • One moment beats a whole deployment. The hour, the conversation, the drive back.
  • If the song is for somebody, tell us who they were.
  • Include the thing that stuck. A smell, a song on the radio, a phrase somebody used. That's usually where the song is hiding.
  • It doesn't have to be combat. Coming home is a story. So is a good chief, a bad year, or the day you took the uniform off for the last time.

What happens to it

  • You see the words before anything is recorded.
  • It may be woven together with another veteran's story so it reaches more of us.
  • No veteran's name is ever attached to a published song.
  • Until the words are final, you can pull it back, no reason required.

Phantom Soldier

Veteran stories, told as songs. You tell the story you want told. If it becomes one, it's released under Phantom Soldier. No veteran's name is ever on it. No cost to the veteran, and everything the music earns is donated to veteran suicide prevention.
