What this is

Bottom line up front

Veterans have stories that don't survive translation. On paper they read like a report. Out loud they stop a room, so we mostly keep them to ourselves. A song can carry one the whole distance. You bring the truth. We handle the words, the music, and the release.

Any branch, any era, any job you held. If your story becomes a song, it might stand on its own or be woven together with another veteran's, so more of us can see ourselves in it. Your name is never on it. How it works   Who's behind this

How it works

Four steps. The story stays in your control the whole way.

  1. 1

    You tell the story

    A paragraph is enough to start.

  2. 2

    A real person writes you back

    Same person the whole way through.

  3. 3

    Words first, then the song

    You read every line and push back until it's right.

  4. 4

    It goes out under one name

    Released as Phantom Soldier. Your name isn't on it. If it helped you, pass it on.

Who's behind this

A civilian listens and it's just another song. A veteran listens and you can see it land. That's the connection: we all know what we're not talking about.

The honest version

Phantom Soldier

Veteran stories, told as songs. You tell the story you want told. If it becomes one, it's released under Phantom Soldier. No veteran's name is ever on it. No cost to the veteran, and everything the music earns is donated to veteran suicide prevention.
